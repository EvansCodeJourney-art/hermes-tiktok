Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how the Hermes TikTok integration project handles information when users connect authorized TikTok accounts through supported third-party services.

Information and Permissions

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the integration may access TikTok account information or content needed to perform a requested task.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to support authorized integration features, such as content workflows and account-related functions requested by the user.

Third-Party Services

Connections may involve TikTok and Composio. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security and Retention

Users should protect their credentials and access tokens. Data handling and retention depend on the services and configuration used. Do not submit sensitive information unless necessary for the feature.

Your Choices

You can stop using the integration and revoke granted permissions through the relevant service settings where supported.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the repository where this project is maintained.
